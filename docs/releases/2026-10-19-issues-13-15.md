## Behavior

- Issue 13 projects published, preview, volunteer and prepared schedule reads onto strictly future session starts in each session's time zone before presentation grouping. Related assignments, backups and shortfalls stay consistent; history remains stored.
- Issue 14 ranks compatible volunteers by readiness, assignments allocated in the proposed run, published continuity and volunteer ID. Backup ordering uses completed proposed workloads. Weekly grouping preserves differing dated staffing.
- Issue 15 adds the supplied calendar setup flow beside assignments. Owner-only management creates, resets and disables encrypted personal credentials. The sessionless feed exposes the owner's current published future assignments with stable event IDs and cancellation tombstones for obsolete future assignments. It does not expose roster details or cancellation reasons.
