## Revised messages

Notifications describe the volunteer and saved change rather than exposing operation identifiers, raw ISO timestamps or revision counters. Subjects distinguish availability exceptions, weekly availability updates and assignment cancellations. Bodies show the affected date or weekday, AM/PM times in Eastern Time, any supplied reason, the submitting account and an appropriate coordinator next step. Cancellation messages use canonical session labels from the same saved snapshot. Cleared weekly availability is stated explicitly.

Example using fictional data:

```text
Subject: Availability updated: Alex Example

Alex Example updated their availability.

Unavailable: Monday, October 19, 2026
Time: 9:00 AM–11:00 AM (Eastern Time)
Reason: Travel plans

Submitted by: alex@example.test

The change is saved. Review the latest availability in the volunteer scheduler.

UMD Mindfulness Society
```

Recipients and the sending account remain unchanged. Rendering and sending happen after commit; either failure is logged without turning a saved mutation into an error. Durable replays continue to return the original response without sending another notification. Date/time formatters initialize only when a notification needs them.
