Mindfulness Society

Volunteer scheduler

Coordinate volunteer availability and staff mindfulness sessions across the society's centers.

What the scheduler does

Volunteers share weekly availability, record dated exceptions, view assignments, and cancel when they cannot attend. Administrators review and publish schedules, import WhenIsGood availability, and monitor staffing. Center contacts propose upcoming session times.

Google sign-in

The scheduler verifies your Google email address and checks it against the society's authorized user roster. Your assigned role determines which scheduling tools you can use.

Scheduler Gmail Notifications

Scheduler Gmail Notifications is the scheduler's email component. The operator authorizes one Gmail account to send administrative notifications about scheduling changes. This authorization requests permission to send email only; it does not request permission to read the account's inbox. Volunteers do not need to authorize Gmail access.

Access and support

The application is intended for authorized society volunteers, administrators, and center contacts. Contact your center organizer or the scheduler administrator for access or help.
